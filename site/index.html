Made for what you copy

One card at your cursor for text, code, data, images and files. Everything runs on your Mac.

Clipboard first

⌃⌥Space opens a card at your cursor, a click on the menu bar icon opens it under the icon. With Keep clipboard history on, it shows your latest copy and keeps 20 copies and 20 pinned items in memory, searchable and with a diff of two copies. Without it, press ⌘V on the card. You can also drop an image, a PDF, a Word file or a zip on it.

Recognises and decodes

JSON, YAML, XML, HTML, Markdown, code (Rust, Java, Python), URLs and CSV/TSV are recognised and formatted. Decodes JWT, UUID, Unix time, ISO 8601, URL, Base64 and hashes, and converts YAML to JSON.

Text menu and snippets

Filter or reverse lines or take a column, clean up URLs, case style, Base64, URL and hex encoding, SHA-256, SHA-1 and MD5, and a QR code. Snippets with fields to fill in, and new UUIDs and timestamps.

Screenshots with text recognition

⌃⌥4 takes a screenshot of an area, a window or the whole screen, straight to the clipboard. The text in it is recognised on your Mac; drag the thumbnail to share it.

Images and documents

Read text and QR codes from images, resize, rotate, flip, grayscale and remove metadata; save as PNG, JPEG or HEIC. Text from PDF (scanned too) and Word, and view and extract a zip.

Table window

Open a CSV or TSV as a table: search, sort, choose columns, remove duplicate and empty rows, with undo. The profile shows missing values, types and outliers. Copy as JSON or CSV, or save as CSV.

Chart, saved as PNG

Choose columns and see them as a chart in the table window. Save the chart as PNG or have the trend summarised in words.

AI on your Mac

With Apple Intelligence: summarise, rewrite, fix spelling and translate (NL↔EN), explain code and explain the structure of JSON or YAML. All on the device; needs a Mac with Apple silicon.

Fully offline

No network access, no account, no analytics, telemetry or crash reports. History is kept in memory only; only snippets you save yourself go to disk. Read the privacy policy.
